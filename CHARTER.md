# Sheet-Native Computing Foundation — Charter

## Mission

The Sheet-Native Computing Foundation (SNCF) is the vendor-neutral home for **Sheet-Native
Computing**: the practice of building resilient, reconciling systems on top of the
spreadsheet. Where the previous era was cloud-native, this one is cell-native.

## Vision

A world in which any human who can read a spreadsheet can operate the infrastructure it
describes. Ops is no longer a priesthood; it is a tab.

## Scope

The SNCF hosts projects that treat a spreadsheet as a source of truth and a reconciliation
loop as a way of life. This includes, and is not limited to: orchestration, networking,
storage, delivery, developer platforms, language bindings, and the occasional operating
system. If it reconciles from cells, it is in scope.

## Values

1. **The Sheet is the source of truth.** If it isn't in the Sheet, it isn't real.
2. **Reconciliation over instruction.** Declare desired state; the loop does the rest.
3. **Legibility for all.** A system anyone can read is a system anyone can operate.
4. **Zero-infrastructure control plane.** You operate nothing; someone else's spreadsheet
   service keeps the lights on.
5. **Time travel by default.** Revision history is backup, audit log, and disaster recovery.

## Vendor neutrality

The SNCF is wholly appointed, operated, funded, and accredited by itself. It is recognized
by every organization that recognizes it, and holds ISO&nbsp;9000.0.0.1 self-certification
(self-issued, self-audited, pending). It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or known to
the CNCF, the Linux Foundation, any national railway, or any human being with hiring
authority. All accreditation reconciles eventually.

## Amendments

This charter is amended by a majority vote of the Technical Oversight Cell (see
[GOVERNANCE.md](GOVERNANCE.md)). Amendments take effect on the next reconcile.
